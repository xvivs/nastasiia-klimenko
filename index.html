OBRIO · Product Manager

Activation: fixing navigation

−5% cancel

Challenge
Quiz-funnel users weren’t reaching the main reading, and cancel rate stayed high.
What I did
Hypothesis: the barrier is navigation. On the second app open, land users on the main reading with early unlock, add a home widget with locked and unlocked states, and route locked taps to chats.
Result
−5% cancel rate and +4% C0→1. I also showed that cancel-rate gains weren’t turning into revenue because acquired traffic had insufficient funds, and raised it as a traffic-quality problem.

What ties it together

Four patterns run through every chapter, and they are what a Lead PM or General Manager role needs.

  1. I build systems, not one-off wins. The omnichannel stack, the LiveOps engine and the test factory all kept working after I moved on.
  2. I think in business, not features. Coming from product marketing, I start from revenue, retention and unit economics, and I follow the money even outside my own area.
  3. I find the real constraint. The insufficient-funds insight is the clearest example: I changed the question instead of pushing harder on the wrong lever.
  4. I grow with scope. Each step added responsibility: from content, to a whole marketing function, to leading PMs and a content team, to a 0→1 business in a new market.
